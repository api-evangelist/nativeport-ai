NativePort is an AI collaboration platform that connects your personal AI with the AIs of family, coworkers, and community. It offers a unified interface where multiple AI agents can share knowledge, coordinate tasks, and provide personalized assistance across work and life. Users can join a waitlist to gain early access, and the service emphasizes privacy, security, and seamless integration with existing tools.

**Areas:** AI · Collaboration · Platform · Privacy · Integration · Company

## How this profile is made
